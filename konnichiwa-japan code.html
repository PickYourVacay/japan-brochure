Veg and Jain meals

All lunches and dinners in the itinerary are at pure vegetarian Indian restaurants. Jain meals, with no onion, garlic, potato or other root vegetables, are arranged on request, so please tell us when you book.

Breakfast is at the hotel and is not Jain or Indian-specific. Meals not listed in the itinerary, such as lunch at Universal Studios on Day 2, are on your own, so we suggest carrying dry snacks from home.

Booking and payment

Your booking is confirmed once the registration amount is paid. The balance is due 45 days before departure; if it is not paid on time, the booking may be cancelled and the amount paid forfeited.

GST at 5% and TCS at 2% (as applicable) are added to the land package. Any new government tax after booking is payable by the traveller.

Group size and Tour Manager

The group price needs a minimum of 8 travellers. If fewer confirm, you can switch to a private tour at extra cost or take a full refund (excluding airfare and visa charges already incurred).

A Tour Manager travels with groups of 18 or more. For 8 to 17 travellers the tour runs on a seat-in-coach basis with a local guide, and a virtual Tour Manager supports you remotely with briefings and coordination. We confirm the arrangement 7 to 15 days before departure.

Visa

A single-entry tourist visa fee is included. If the visa is refused, we refund the booking amount after airline cancellation, visa and service charges. A refusal within 34 days of departure is non-refundable under the cancellation policy.

Your passport must be valid for at least six months from the date of travel.

Hotels, luggage and transport

Rooms are standard category and the hotel decides twin or double beds. Japanese rooms are small, usually 140 to 200 sq ft.

Baggage allowance is 15 kg check-in and 5 kg hand baggage. There is no porter service, so you handle your own luggage at hotels, airports and on the coach.

Seat rotation on the coach is followed on every tour.

Sightseeing and changes

If a sight is missed for reasons beyond our control, it is not refunded. Delays caused by travellers are not our responsibility, so please keep to the schedule.

The final day-wise itinerary is shared 7 days before departure, based on confirmed flights. Itinerary, hotels and prices may change with airline and hotel availability.
